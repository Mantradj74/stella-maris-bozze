Lanzarote

An island you get to know slowly.

Black sand, red craters and César Manrique's white architecture, in a UNESCO Biosphere Reserve, with mild winters and gentle summers. With months instead of days, living in Lanzarote stops being a list of places and becomes your life: a swim before work, a hike on a free afternoon, Sunday lunch in a fishing village.

Puerto Calero

Puerto Calero: your neighbourhood by the marina.

Puerto Calero marina, a small harbour on the south coast, a short drive from Puerto del Carmen: a morning walk along the boats, cafés and restaurants by the water, sailing and sea outings from the dock. Calm all year, and the feeling of living somewhere, not passing through.

FAQ

The questions we hear most.

Anything else on your mind? Send a request and our team will answer you personally.

Can I stay for just one month?

Yes. Stays start at one month and can last up to twelve. Tell us the months you have in mind and we reply with availability for each residence.

What is included in the monthly rate?

Your private residence, regular housekeeping, high-speed Starlink, air conditioning and heating, the two infinity pools, hot tubs, the gym and a team on site. The full list is in Your stay includes, and every proposal comes with clear, written terms.

Is the connection good enough for remote work?

Every residence has high-speed Starlink, stable enough for video calls and large files. The Canary Islands share the time zone of London and Lisbon, so your meetings keep their usual hours.

Is Villa Stella Maris adults only?

Yes. Only adults live here, across fourteen residences: the villa stays calm all day, ideal for working, resting and living at your own pace.

Can a company arrange a stay for a team or a film crew?

Yes. We work with companies and productions that need several residences for weeks or months: one contact for everything, flexible dates and complete discretion. Just tell us the number of people and the period in the request form.

What is the weather like in winter?

Mild. January afternoons average around 21 °C, and in August they stay below 30 °C. The Atlantic is around 19 °C in winter, so a swim is never out of season.

What kind of contract do you use?

A seasonal rental contract under Spanish law (contrato de temporada), for stays of one to twelve months. Before signing you receive everything in writing: dates, rate and what is included.

How far is Puerto Calero from the airport?

Lanzarote Airport is about 15 km away, roughly 15 to 20 minutes by car. Arrecife, the island's capital, and Puerto del Carmen are also close by.
